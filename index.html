About

Saba(she/they) is a Paris-based multidisciplinary artist whose work bridges visual arts, performance, design, and the written word. Originally from Pakistan and raised through formative years in Hong Kong, she has spent over a decade in Paris weaving her cross-cultural perspective into evocative creative practices. She grew up immersed in performance and visual craft; singing in choirs, playing instruments, and discovering a passion for the stage through early theatrical roles ranging from a mythical Troll to Felicity Morgan in Robert Swindells' Room 13. This early spark ignited a lifelong dedication to storytelling across mediums. Holding a Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design from LISAA Paris, Saba channels a rich, varied background into filmmaking, poetry, and fine art. Her visual work centers on emotive portraiture and raw sketches, delving into raw human vulnerability to explore and give voice to stigmatized or shunned emotions.
